SlideForge Privacy Notice
Version: 2026-04-20

1. Scope
This notice describes how SlideForge handles data processed through the application.

2. Data You Provide
You may provide:
- Slide decks and uploaded source documents
- Guardrail templates and audit inputs
- Configuration values for model providers

3. Processing Model
SlideForge can operate with local or remote model providers.
- Local providers process content on your machine or local network.
- Remote providers may receive content according to your configuration.

4. Local Storage
SlideForge stores operational data locally, including session state, logs, history, and generated artifacts.

5. Third-Party Processing
If you configure external providers, their privacy and retention policies apply to requests sent to them.

6. Security
SlideForge includes safeguards, but no system is perfectly secure. You are responsible for your endpoint, network, and access controls.

7. Data Retention and Deletion
Retention depends on your local environment and operational workflows. You are responsible for deletion and lifecycle management of local artifacts.

8. Sensitive Data
Do not upload data unless you have proper authorization and legal basis.

9. International Transfers
When using third-party providers, data may be processed in other jurisdictions.

10. Updates
This notice may change over time. The version date indicates the current revision.
